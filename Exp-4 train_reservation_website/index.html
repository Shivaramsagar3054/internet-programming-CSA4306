Frequently asked questions

How do I book a ticket?

Log in, search for trains, select a train and class, enter passenger details, and confirm the demo payment.

What is a PNR?

A Passenger Name Record (PNR) is a unique reference for a reservation. This demo creates a sample PNR for each booking.

Can I cancel a ticket and get a refund?

You can request cancellation using your PNR. The demo calculates an illustrative refund according to its sample rules.

Are payments processed?

No. Payments are simulated. A real railway booking service needs an approved payment gateway and secure backend.
